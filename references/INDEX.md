## Book Catalog

### hormozi-100m
- **Title**: $100M Offers: How to Make Offers So Good People Feel Stupid Saying No
- **Author**: Alex Hormozi
- **File**: `100m-offers_alex-hormozi.pdf`
- **Summary**: Framework for creating irresistible offers through value stacking, pricing psychology, and eliminating buyer risk.
- **Key concepts**: Grand Slam Offer, value equation (dream outcome x perceived likelihood / time delay x effort & sacrifice), pricing to value not cost, bonuses & guarantees, scarcity & urgency, naming & packaging
- **Best for**: Offer creation, pricing strategy, increasing perceived value, reducing churn through better packaging
- **Skill dimension**: Business Model, Positioning

### hard-thing
- **Title**: The Hard Thing About Hard Things: Building a Business When There Are No Easy Answers
- **Author**: Ben Horowitz
- **File**: `the-hard-thing-about-hard-things_ben-horowitz.pdf`
- **Summary**: Raw lessons from a CEO who navigated near-death startup experiences — firing friends, managing debt, pivoting under pressure.
- **Key concepts**: Wartime vs peacetime CEO, the struggle, hiring executives, managing your psychology, giving feedback, training your people, minimizing politics
- **Best for**: Leadership crises, hard decisions, managing through downturns, scaling teams, founder psychology
- **Skill dimension**: Founder Fit, Risk Assessment

### intelligent-investor
- **Title**: The Intelligent Investor: A Book of Practical Counsel
- **Author**: Benjamin Graham
- **File**: `the-intelligent-investor_benjamin-graham.pdf`
- **Summary**: The bible of value investing — margin of safety, Mr. Market metaphor, defensive vs enterprising investor approaches.
- **Key concepts**: Margin of safety, Mr. Market, intrinsic value, defensive investor, enterprising investor, dollar-cost averaging, market fluctuations vs risk
- **Best for**: Investment decisions, understanding market psychology, valuation principles, long-term wealth building
- **Skill dimension**: Financial Projections

### financial-intelligence
- **Title**: Financial Intelligence: A Manager's Guide to Knowing What the Numbers Really Mean
- **Author**: Karen Berman, John Case, Joe Knight
- **File**: `financial-intelligence_karen-berman-joe-knight.pdf`
- **Summary**: Plain-English guide to financial statements, ratios, and what the numbers actually tell you about a business.
- **Key concepts**: Income statement, balance sheet, cash flow, profit vs cash, ROI, working capital, ratios (gross margin, operating margin, ROIC), art of finance (estimates & assumptions)
- **Best for**: Reading financials, unit economics, understanding burn rate, evaluating business health, due diligence
- **Skill dimension**: Financial Projections, Business Model

### dotcom-secrets

- **Author**: Russell Brunson
- **File**: `dotcom-secrets_russell-brunson.pdf`
- **Summary**: Blueprint for building sales funnels, crafting hooks, and converting traffic into customers online.
- **Key concepts**: Value ladder, secret formula (who/where/bait/result), attractive character, soap opera sequences, funnel types (lead squeeze, tripwire, webinar, product launch), traffic temperature (hot/warm/cold)
- **Best for**: Marketing funnels, lead generation, email sequences, conversion optimization, online sales strategy
- **Skill dimension**: GTM Strategy, Positioning, Content Strategy
